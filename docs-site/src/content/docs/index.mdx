## About TacoFloor

TacoFloor gives a restaurant team a real-time view of the floor during service, with role-based controls split along accountability lines — waiters open tables and take orders, but only managers can register or approve a payment.

[See the live demo →](https://alina-desya.github.io/tacofloor)
